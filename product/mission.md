# Product Mission

## Problem

Touring live entertainment professionals — tour managers, production managers, engineers, backline techs, lighting and video crew, drivers, wardrobe, and hundreds of adjacent roles — find work entirely through personal relationships and informal channels like WhatsApp group chats. There is no trusted, searchable, vetted space where the industry's working crew can signal real-time availability, post urgent crew needs, share hard-won road knowledge, or build a verifiable career record. LinkedIn is built for corporate careers. Generic job boards have no context for how touring works. The real conversations happen in private rooms that don't exist yet — so AWN is building them.

## Target Users

**Primary — Touring Crew (free forever)**
The ~200 roles spanning 18 departments that make a tour run: tour managers, production managers, FOH/monitor engineers, backline techs, lighting designers, video crew, wardrobe stylists, bus drivers, tour accountants, security, PAs, and more. Specifically the working professionals — those doing stadium runs down to club tours — who lack a professional home on the internet that actually understands their world.

**Secondary — Employers (paid)**
Production companies, artist management offices, tour managers with staffing budgets, festivals, and venues looking to source vetted, available crew fast. They pay for access to the network; crew never do.

## Solution

AWN is a private, invite-only professional network built exclusively for touring live entertainment crew. It is the vetted room where real touring conversations happen:

- **Real-time availability** — members signal when they're on the road or open for work, surfaced as a live pulse the whole network can see
- **Help Now** — urgent 48-hour crew callouts pushed directly to relevant members with the right role and availability
- **Peer-verified credits** — tour credits endorsed by colleagues who were actually there, creating a trust graph of who worked with whom
- **Road Intel / Atlas** — crowdsourced venue notes, vendor recommendations, lifestyle tips, and industry contacts accumulated across every market
- **The List** — a curated, community-maintained database of industry contacts
- **Private messaging** — DMs and discipline-specific rooms (audio, lighting, video, etc.) for the real conversations

The strategic moat is trust: every member is manually approved, credits require peer vouches, and the community stays invite-only. Crew are free forever. Employers pay to access the talent.
